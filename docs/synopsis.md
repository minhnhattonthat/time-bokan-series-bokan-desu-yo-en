# Synopsis

Time Bokan Series: Bokan desu yo (PS1, 1998, Banpresto / Eleven). A vertical shooter and slapstick parody: the player is
the villains. The Doronbow trio (Doronjo, Boyacky, Tonzra) fly the villains' mecha from all the Time Bokan shows against
that series' heroes. Everything spoken is in the 16 FMVs; the rest is labels, stage cards, credits and lyric cards.

Two routes, from the script tables in `docs/re/state.md`. The Extras list names MOVIE01-15 in order. MOVIE00 is the
opening: title call, theme song, Dokurobey's sponsor joke.

**Normal Game** (the trio work for themselves): MOVIE01, ST1, 02, ST2, 03, ST3, 04, ST4, 05, ST5, 06, 07, ending.
1. MOVIE01 "Prologue A": Doronjo dreams of Yatterman telling her to retire. Boyacky has sent out challenge letters.
2. MOVIE02-04 "Getting Cocky": they win stage after stage and beat the giant robots.
3. MOVIE05 "The Good Guys' Challenge": all heroes seem beaten and GREAT VICTORY goes up, then Yatterman returns.
4. MOVIE06 "Settled by Self-Destruct": the giant bomb's button fails and it goes wrong. Lost again.
5. MOVIE07 "Ending": tired Doronjo is cheered up by her men and decides to go on a world rampage.

**Special Game** (Dokurobey hires them): MOVIE08, ST1, 09, ST2, 10, STS, 11, ST3, 12, ST4, 05, ST5, 13, STF, 14, 07,
ending, 15.
1. MOVIE08 "Prologue B": Dokurobey orders them to beat every hero, promising top billing; they daydream of stardom.
2. MOVIE09 and MOVIE12 pep talks, MOVIE10 the special training stage, MOVIE11 the Dokurobey Car as a dubious reward.
3. MOVIE13 "Dokurobey's Betrayal": with the heroes down he'll star alone in "Time Dokurobey". They storm his base.
4. Final stage, then MOVIE14 "The Good Guys Attack!": they won, and Boyacky calls a spare owl mecha (voiced by the
   composer himself). Yatterman return: they only pretended to lose, to draw out Dokurobey. They let the trio off.
5. MOVIE07, then MOVIE15 "Punishment Time!": Dokurobey, grudgingly impressed, punishes them anyway. Song.

Late reveals to protect: Dokurobey's betrayal (MOVIE13), and that Yatterman threw the earlier fight (MOVIE14). No early
line hints at either.

Running jokes: Dokurobey's punishment; Boyacky's button press; "Flatter a pig and up the tree it goes"; the trio's wish
for a major debut as stars; the heroes' lecture that they are too old.
