# Style reference

Subtitles are short and carry no speaker names, so each voice must show in the line itself.

| Speaker | JP markers | English devices | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Doronjo | あたしゃ, だよ, じゃないか, お前たち, かい | bossy, sharp; "you two", "boys", "Let's...!" | 今すぐ出撃するんだよ! → We sortie right now! |
| Boyacky | ボクちゃん, ですよ, はい, ドクちゃん, ポチッとな | fawning and smug; "little ol' me", "yes indeed", "Doku-chan", "Annnd... click!" | 準備は整いましたです、はい → All preparations are complete, yes indeed. |
| Tonzra | わい, まんねん, でっせ, や, ほんま (Kansai) | rough, dropped g; "I tell ya", "ain't", "goin'" | ちゃんと配ってきたまんねん → Delivered 'em all, I tell ya! |
| Dokurobey | わし, だべ, べぇ, スカポンタン | rustic and pompous; ", y'hear", "I reckon", "you numbskulls" | お仕置きで償ってもらうべー → ...they'll pay with a punishment, y'hear! |
| Yatterman-1 | 俺, だ, 参上 | clean-cut boy hero, plain declaratives | この世に悪は栄えない! → ...evil shall never prosper in this world! |
| Yatterman-2 | わ, なさい, でしょ | teasing, big-sister | いつまでも若くないんだから → You're not getting any younger, you know. |
| Owl mecha | でね, がね (Nagoya) | ", ya know" | 待ちに待っとった出番が来たがね → My long-awaited turn has come at last, ya know! |

Rules:
- One idea per subtitle. Aim at 2 lines at most, 272 px each. `tools/tl_check.py` is the gate.
- A row split across a pause reads across it with "..." at both ends.
- Catchphrases never change: "Arahora-sassa!", "Annnd... click!", "punishment time".
- Puns get a new pun, never a note: 流石 misread three ways → "Genius, genie-us, gee, what a genius!"; fukuro/ofukuro →
  "Hoo-hoo, owl right! Mom's in sight!".
- Stage cards end on the announcer's tic: Omotchama "Koron!", Perasuke "Peccha!".
- Row 赤ポンタン in Whisper's raw reading is Dokurobey's スカポンタン. Corrected example: not "you red pontan", but "you
  numbskulls".
- Lyrics are never translated line by line (D10): a card gets an English title and a short summary in our own words.
